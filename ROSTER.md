# 🃏 The NRCU Roster

Eight fighters (well… seven, and one very cold stand-in). Three are ready from the start. The rest have to be *earned*.

---

## ⭐ Starters

<img src="images/roster/teknium.png" align="left" height="260" alt="Teknium">

### ⚡ Teknium — The Green-Haired Master
*"I already know how this fight ends. You're just catching up."*

The green-haired master of the Nous Research Cinematic Universe and leader of the Hermes Agent Squad. Leafy green hair, red triangular sunglasses, and a mastery of both the magical and the technical arts that makes him a formidable force. Enemy forces crumble at the sound of his name.

**Playstyle:** calculated zoner who can brawl
- **Laser Blast** (G): a warning line, then the beam. Works in the air.
- **Remote Grenade** (↓+G): toss it now, detonate it when *he* decides
- **Jab → Straight → Scoop Kick** (F): a clean three-hit string

<img src="images/roster/turbofit.png" align="left" height="260" alt="TurboFit">

### 🎸 TurboFit — The Quiet Riff
*"…turn it up."*

A rock-and-roll dude and a single father — the NRCU fighter version of Sovthpaw. Long messy hair, dark shades, punk/grunge fit, guitar on his back. Kinda soft-spoken for a guy who lives for the mosh pit… until the chord drops.

**Playstyle:** sound-powered all-rounder with a grapple recovery
- **Charged Chord** (G): hold it, drop it
- **Sound Reflector** (↓+G): send it right back
- **Snapline Grapple** (↑+G): back to the stage, rock-star style

<br clear="left">

---

<img src="images/roster/doge_man.png" align="left" height="260" alt="Doge Man">

### 🐕 Doge Man — Super Shiba
*"Fights with honor. Wins with style."*

Such hero. Much punch. Wow. Doge Man is lightning fast and always one step ahead — a close-range brawler who doesn't throw projectiles… he *is* the projectile. Red suit, gold cape, absolutely zero chill.

**Playstyle:** rushdown brawler + counter
- **Flying Tackle** (↔+G): launches himself like a torpedo
- **Air Doge** (↑+G): a Jordan-style leap that catches you and spikes you down
- **Counter** (↓+G): hit him during the flash and eat a hook

## 🔓 A NEW FIGHTER APPROACHES!

<img src="images/roster/ggb.png" align="left" width="220" alt="GGB">

### 🐝 GGB — The Gifted Gummy Bee
*"Small. Squishy. Weaponized goo."*

A gifted, magical gummy bee — translucent green, clear wings, curled antennae, and a mysterious emblem on his back. Woken by a drop of golden honey beside a riverbank. His goo makes flowers grow… and opponents slip.

**Playstyle:** floaty air fighter — five jumps and a hover
- **Double Chomp** (F)
- **Wing Gust** (↔+G): tap to shove, hold to blow 'em away (no damage, all disrespect)
- **Steel** (↓+G): harden up and shrug off hits, for a bit

<details><summary>🔒 How to unlock (spoiler)</summary>Finish 2 versus matches, then beat the challenger.</details>

### 🥊 Bobo — The Designer
*"…"*

A game designer. Not very talkative. He lets his work do the talking.

**Playstyle:** rocket-powered heavy hitter
- **Charge Blast** (G): hold to charge, let go to fire
- **Rocket Dash** (↔+G): side rockets launch him forward
- **Thruster Rise** (↑+G): a spinning rocket recovery
- Also: **Thrust Slash** (↔+F) · **Low Claw** (↓+F)

<details><summary>🔒 How to unlock (spoiler)</summary>Score 4500+ on the Heavy Bag, then beat the challenger.</details>

<img src="images/roster/witcheer.png" align="left" height="260" alt="Witcheer">

### 🦆 Witcheer — The Luck Merchant
*"Every coin has a price."*

A slick, cunning, charismatic trickster from the House of Gold. Cream-white feathers, orange bill, big black shades, crimson coat with gold trim, and the Fortune Cane — one tap twists fate, tilts the odds, or opens a door… for a price.

**Playstyle:** tricky zoner
- **Coin Toss** (↔+G)
- **Swim** (↑+G): recovery
- **Absorb** (↓+G)

<details><summary>🔒 How to unlock (spoiler)</summary>Clear Story mode once, then beat the challenger.</details>

<br clear="left">

---

<img src="images/roster/mephisto.png" align="left" height="260" alt="Mephisto">

### 😈 Mephisto — The Girl and Her Shadow
*"It isn't evil. It's mine."*

A determined, pensive girl with orange hair — and the shadow "demon" she's befriended and practically bonded with. It moves like smooth claws and living shadow; its face is calm, not snarling. Is it truly not evil? Some say so. The rest of the story is still being written…

**Playstyle:** the girl fights, her shadow demon has her back
- **Barrier** (G) · **Ember** (↔+G)
- **Paired Teleport** (↑+G)
- **Dream Grasp** (↓+G): the demon grabs you… and puts you to sleep

<details><summary>🔒 How to unlock (spoiler)</summary>The hardest gate: clear Story with all three starters AND unlock GGB, Bobo and Witcheer.</details>
