# NRCU v0.6.1 — unfinished alpha tester

> An unfinished alpha for friends and community testers. Please expect rough edges and report what breaks.

## What's new

- **TurboFit's original look returns**, with corrected standing arms and guitar grip, four selectable colors, and matching transparent character-select portraits. His separate results-screen clap is still here.
- **Cleaner costume selection:** transparent Teknium, Witcheer and TurboFit portraits; Amber Honey GGB keeps his translucent wings without the grey rectangle.
- **New Home and two-step setup:** choose fighters, costumes and devices, then choose a stage. Back preserves your lineup.
- **Doge Man:** tap grounded neutral Special for Super Doge Punch. His counter and Air Doge remain separate directional specials.
- **Witcheer:** cane swipe, rising cane, low poke and held crouch, with smoother return to animated idle/crouch. Side basics remain kicks.
- **Bobo:** a dedicated Down+Attack aerial, separate from grounded Low Claw. Down+Special is not a move.
- **Mephisto:** current ground/air basics and Meteor Heel are connected to playable routes; Story uses the current fighter factory.
- **Teknium:** planted low-sweep/crouch handling and a provisional descending-stamp down-air. Laser Blast remains fresh-use with no stored charge.
- **Hermes Weaver:** a revised gap-floor stage and repair cycle. Required model setup now also runs correctly in release builds.
- **A smaller browser build:** high-quality color-texture compression brings the complete site below its hosting limit. Original source art, normal maps, metallic/roughness textures and portrait transparency are retained.

## Controls

| Device | Move / aim | Jump | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 keyboard | WASD | Space or W | F | G |
| P2 keyboard | Arrow keys | Enter or Up | K | L |
| Gamepad | Stick or D-pad | A | X | B |
| Touch | On-screen pad | Up | Attack | Special |

Direction changes the move. There is no universal shield. Esc pauses; R after a winner rematches. Connect controllers before launch.

**TurboFit's held Sound Orb:** on solid ground, hold Down+Special; release either button to end it. Being interrupted or leaving the ground also ends it. It pushes opponents and reflects reflectable projectiles, not every melee attack.

**Teknium's Laser Blast:** a fresh jump or fresh Special press before the white flash cancels; no charge is stored.

## Please test and report

Try menus, costume cycling, stage Back, ordinary combat, results/rematch and Story. Check both facings, ground versus air, fresh taps versus holds, and interruption. On phones, turn sideways and try the touch controls, including holding and releasing TurboFit's orb. Report device/browser, stage, fighters/costumes, exact buttons and what happened. Screenshots or a short clip help.

## Known issues / coverage

- Unfinished alpha: art, balance and performance still need feedback. Teknium's down-air remains provisional.
- Local automated checks cover the current release routes. Windows and desktop-browser playback reached ordinary four-slot combat, natural results, rematch, pause/leave and Bobo Story entry. A full natural Story clear, every matchup/stage, physical controllers and long sessions are not certified.
- One earlier uncompressed-source early-close test emitted headless renderer errors. Subsequent comparison runs and the final optimized suite passed; no root-cause fix is claimed.
- Real-phone acceptance is deferred to post-publication testing and remains **unverified**. Touch support and desktop-browser checks are not proof of performance on a physical phone.
