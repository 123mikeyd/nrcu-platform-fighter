## 🔥 What is this?

**NRCU Platform Fighter** is a playable alpha for Mike's Nous Research Cinematic Universe fighter: a fast, ridiculous, community-character brawler built in Godot.

Pick a starter, fight friends or CPUs, unlock challengers, run Story mode, chase Heavy Bag scores, and please report whatever breaks in the funniest way possible.

## 🎮 Controls

<img src="images/controls.png" alt="Teknium's Dojo: how to play NRCU with an Xbox controller, keyboard, or phone" width="100%">

**P1:** WASD move/aim · Space jump · **F** basic · **G** special<br>
**P2:** Arrows · Enter jump · **K** basic · **L** special<br>
**Gamepad:** supported. Direction + attack changes your move. Esc pauses.

---

## 🕹️ Modes

- **Versus:** up to 4 fighters — friends, CPUs, teams, chaos.
- **Story:** win encounters, reveal locked challengers, and clear the run.
- **Heavy Bag:** 15 seconds to break the bag and flex your score.

## ✨ v0.6.2 browser alpha — Legends, barks and Story movies

Updated Doge attacks and punch effects, Story movies and credits, TurboFit clap costume colors, and a lossless texture-payload reduction. The complete browser download is still about 911 MB before transport compression. **[Read the browser patch notes →](PATCH_NOTES_v0.6.2.md)**

This release updates the browser game; the Windows button above still downloads v0.6.1. Real-phone performance and long-session stability need tester feedback. No FPS improvement is claimed from the smaller download.

## Previously in v0.6.1 — Pick your color. Bring the chaos.

**TurboFit’s original look returns.** Four colors, corrected guitar grip, and the results-screen clap you know. Transparent costume portraits make the lineup clearer; the new two-step setup lets you choose your fighters first, then your stage.

- **Fresh fights:** Super Doge Punch, Witcheer’s cane basics and crouch, Bobo’s down-air, and current Mephisto ground/air routes.
- **Mind the gaps:** Hermes Weaver’s revised floor and repair cycle are ready for playtesting.
- **Browser first:** a smaller color-texture payload, with source art and portrait transparency preserved. The initial download is still large.

**[Read the v0.6.1 patch notes and move tips →](PATCH_NOTES_v0.6.1.md)**

> **Unfinished alpha, not a finished beta.** Desktop Windows/browser playbacks have been checked. Real-phone testing is deferred until after publication; physical gamepads, long sessions and a full natural Story clear remain unverified. Teknium’s down-air is provisional.

This is an alpha. If you fall through the floor, unlock somebody in a cursed way, get launched into orbit, or discover the funniest bug possible, tell Mike.

- what mode you were in
- who you were playing
- browser or Windows build
- what happened right before it broke
