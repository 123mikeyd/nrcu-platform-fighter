# 📜 PATCH v0.6 — LEDGES ARE HERE!

*The fighters finally learned how to hold on.*

The biggest NRCU update yet: ledges, unlockable challengers, a whole new way to finish Story, a punching-bag mini-game, new moves for almost everybody… and a lot fewer fighters falling to their doom for no reason.

---

## 🧗 Ledge Grab
Miss the stage by a hair? Not anymore.
- **Teknium, Doge Man and TurboFit** now auto-grab ledges.
- From the ledge: **↑ / toward** to climb up · **Space** to jump · **↓** to let go.
- TurboFit hangs a little lower than the others, so his ledge jump gets a little extra kick.

## 🔓 A NEW FIGHTER APPROACHES!
Not everyone shows up on day one anymore.
- **Starters:** Teknium, TurboFit and Doge Man.
- **GGB, Bobo, Witcheer and Mephisto** have to be earned. Meet the secret condition, survive a challenger fight, and they join your roster.
- Mephisto is the hardest one to unlock. Good luck. 🌘
- Want hints? They're hidden as spoilers on the [roster page](ROSTER.md).

## 🎬 Story Mode Glow-Up
- **VS splash** before every fight. Locked fighters show up as a mysterious **???** silhouette… until the reveal.
- **Smash-style victory screen** after every Story win: the winner poses on the podium, and everyone else claps (whether they like it or not).
- A brand-new **ending** and **credits** roll when you finish the run.

## 🥊 NEW MODE: Heavy Bag
15 seconds. One bag. How hard can you hit?
- Break through the layers, shatter the core, and chase your best score with every fighter.
- …someone is watching that high score. 👀

## 🏆 Victory Screens
- Every winner gets a pose, and the losers clap for them.
- **Teknium** wins with one of **four random victory dances**. You'll just have to win a lot to see them all.

---

## ⚔️ Fighter Updates

### ⚡ Teknium
- **NEW Laser Blast** (G): a warning line, then the beam. Works in the air. Jump or press G again before the white flash to cancel.
- **NEW basic string** (F): jab → straight → scoop kick.
- Ledge grab · second-jump **soft ring**.

### 🐕 Doge Man
- **NEW 6-hit boxing combo** (F): five quick punches and a launching hook. Faster startup after playtesting.
- After his up-special he now drops in a **helpless skydiver fall**. Commit wisely.
- Ledge grab · second-jump **dust puff**.

### 🎸 TurboFit
- **NEW Snapline Grapple** (↑+G): a grapple recovery to get back to the stage, rock-star style.
- **Fresh new look:** updated model with hair that actually moves.
- Ledge grab · second-jump **double whoosh**.

### 🐝 GGB
- **NEW Wing Gust** (↔+G): tap for a quick shove, hold to blow opponents away. Zero damage, maximum disrespect.
- **Fixed:** GGB no longer gets stuck balancing on other fighters' heads.

### ❄️ Ice Mage (Story)
- Full movement kit: jump, a frosty double jump, **Frost Rise** up-special, and proper hurt/tumble/landing animations. He's still CPU-only, holding the frosty slot until a real ice fighter shows up.

### 🌘 Mephisto
- **NEW Dream Grasp** (↓+G): her shadow guardian reaches out and lulls the opponent to sleep.

## 🛠️ Fixes & Quality of Life
- **Pause** with Esc, and flip on **Dev Mode** in Options if you're curious. Dev Mode shows move text and unlocks everyone.
- Winners now stand properly **on** the victory podium instead of sinking into it or floating above it.
- Hurtboxes are now fitted to each fighter's actual body shape.
- Old debug hotkeys and leftover prototype junk removed.
