# v0.6.2 — Legends browser tester

## What's new
- The Legends of Nous Research branding.
- Doge's all-fours bark, aerial dropkick update, and fist-following Super Doge Punch effects.
- Story movies and expanded ending credits.
- TurboFit's legacy clap costume colors.
- Lossless sharing of identical compiled texture payloads reduces this updated build by about 96.7 MB. No roster cuts or texture-resolution reductions.

## Controls
P1: WASD, Space to jump, F attack, G special. P2: arrows, Enter to jump, K attack, L special. Esc pauses. Direction plus attack changes the move. Landscape touch controls remain experimental; use the on-screen pad, Attack and Special.

## Please test and report
Try Local Match, Doge's attacks, Story transitions and movies, costume colors, and touch cancellation/rotation. Tell Mike your device/browser, mode, fighter, and what happened immediately before a problem. The first browser download remains large (about 911 MB before transport compression); keep the tab open.

## Known issues and coverage
This is an unfinished tester alpha. Desktop browser smoke tests and focused automated checks passed. Real-phone performance, physical gamepads, long sessions and a full natural Story clear are not certified. Accelerated Story-transition test fixtures produce WebGL buffer warnings and an exit diagnostic on both ordinary and optimized builds; these remain under investigation. Download-size savings are not an FPS improvement claim.

This is a browser update only. The Windows download remains the earlier v0.6.1 tester. Existing browser save identity is retained.
